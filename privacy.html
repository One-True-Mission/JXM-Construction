PrivacyPolicy.

How JXM Construction LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: June 3, 2026 Last Updated: June 3, 2026

JXM Construction LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at jxmconstructionllc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our Site or contact us about our construction services.

By using this Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Project location (city and state)
  • Type of service requested
  • Project details you choose to share

We collect this information only when you voluntarily provide it. We do not require an account to browse the Site, and we do not collect financial information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry about construction services
  • Provide estimates, quotes, or project information
  • Schedule consultations or site visits
  • Communicate with you about ongoing projects
  • Follow up on past project work or warranty questions

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for marketing purposes beyond responding to your own inquiry.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party service that handles form submissions on our behalf. When you submit the form, your information is transmitted to Formspree and forwarded to us via email. Formspree's handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server log information (such as IP addresses and browser type) as part of providing hosting services. GitHub's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy statement.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. We do not track your browsing behavior across other websites. The only browser storage we use is for remembering your language preference (English or Spanish) when you visit our Site.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and project communications for as long as necessary to provide our services and maintain business records. You may request that we delete your information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, including using secure third-party services for form handling and email. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information from our records
  • Opt out of future communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
